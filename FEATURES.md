# Explore Worldkiln

**Build your world. Give it life. Make it yours.**

Worldkiln combines visual world building with animation, physics, effects, and AeoScript gameplay. Start with a small scene or work toward a larger game, using the tools that fit your idea.

[Get Worldkiln](https://github.com/Aeowun/Worldkiln/releases) · [Getting Started](https://aeowun.com/worldkiln/getting-started/) · [User Guide](DIVE.md)

> **BETA features are already included.** You can use them in Worldkiln today. The BETA label simply means that a feature is still being refined and may have bugs or limitations.

## Environments that react

### Reactive oceans and shorelines · BETA

Bring beaches, coastlines, and open water into your scene. Waves move toward shore, while objects in the water create ripples, wakes, and foam. Wet-looking surfaces change as water washes over them and recedes.

### Underwater atmosphere · BETA

Underwater effects follow the animated water surface as the camera moves through the scene. Create submerged views that feel like part of the same World, rather than a separate flat visual layer.

### Terrain and landscaping · BETA

Generate terrain, sculpt hills and valleys, smooth and flatten areas, paint ground materials, and give your environment natural-looking slopes and shorelines. Add grass and beach materials where they belong.

### Lighting, skies, and materials

Create a visual style with sky and surface materials, water appearance, lights, and distance fog. Use supported Material layers to blend and shape a scene's colors and response to light. Newer material-authoring tools are labeled BETA.

## Worlds you can build in

### Cells and structures

Construct buildings, platforms, interiors, and other shapes with Cells. Work with selections, colors, materials, placement tools, copy and paste, and Undo/Redo.

### Actors and animated characters

Import compatible GLB models, place Actors in your World, and bring animated Characters into Play mode. Set up transforms, physics, and gameplay behavior, and attach props to supported Character rig points.

### Project assets and packs

Use the Asset Manager and Content Browser to organize project-owned content, find available packs, and bring compatible models, textures, audio, and other media into your game. Supported asset fields accept drag-and-drop assignments.

## Games that do something

### AeoScript

Create interactions and gameplay in Worldkiln's own scripting language. AeoScript can work with input, events, Actors, Cells, physics, UI, audio, animations, controllers, and cameras.

Use top-level scripts for game systems, scripts attached to objects for behavior, and Modules to share reusable code. Terrain queries, Actor tags, property-change observers, and selected Material animations are among the newer BETA scripting features.

[Read the AeoScript guide](AEOSCRIPT.md)

### Physics and navigation

Let objects move and collide in Play mode. Add character movement, collision interactions, pathfinding for Actors, and navigation-based behavior within your World.

### Game UI and audio

Build interfaces with Panels, Text, and Buttons. Connect UI to your scripts, play 3D sounds through Audio Emitters, and bring your game to life with animation and supported particle effects.

### Saved game progress

Use AeoScript's `save` features to store supported gameplay values between sessions. Game progress stays separate from the World you've authored in the editor.

## Build, play, share

Switch to **Play mode** whenever you want to try a character, interaction, camera, or game mechanic. Continue building and refining your scene, then use **Build Game** to package a standalone Windows game.

Worldkiln is where you make the game. Your game can stand on its own.
